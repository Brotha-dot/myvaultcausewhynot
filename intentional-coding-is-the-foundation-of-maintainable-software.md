# Intentional coding is the foundation of maintainable software

Maintainable code is the result of intentional choices made by a developer who understands the trade-offs. AI assistance often introduces a layer of "accidental" code—choices made by the model's training data that may not be the best fit for the specific project's constraints. Without [[intentional-coding-is-the-foundation-of-maintainable-software|intentionality]], the codebase slowly drifts toward [[over-reliance-on-ai-leads-to-architectural-blindness|architectural blindness]].
