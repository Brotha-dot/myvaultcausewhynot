# Maintaining Contextual Integrity in Personal Notes

One of the primary challenges in personal knowledge management is the loss of context. Notes taken today may be incomprehensible a year from now if the original context—why the note was taken, what it was related to, and what the thinker's state of mind was—is missing. Arango emphasizes the importance of "contextual integrity," which involves capturing enough metadata and relationship information to ensure that a note remains useful and interpretable in the future.

Maintaining contextual integrity requires a balance between low-friction capture and deliberate organization. While we want to capture ideas quickly, we also need to invest time in linking those ideas to existing knowledge and adding descriptive metadata. This process of "pivoting" between notes using semantic links creates a web of meaning that preserves the context of each individual piece of information within the larger system.
