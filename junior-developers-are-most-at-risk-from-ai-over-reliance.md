# Junior developers are most at risk from AI over-reliance

Early-career developers need to build their foundational skills through repetitive practice and struggle. If they use AI to bypass these stages, they may reach "senior" titles without ever developing the deep technical intuition and problem-solving abilities that define true seniority. This [[skill-atrophy-is-a-silent-cost-of-automated-assistance|skill atrophy]] can be a career-long handicap.
