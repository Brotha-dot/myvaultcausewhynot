# The Extended Mind

The Extended Mind challenges the traditional view that thinking happens only inside the head. Annie Murphy Paul explores the "extra-neural" resources we use to think—our bodies, our physical environments, and our social connections. The book synthesizes research from cognitive science, psychology, and education to show how we can enhance our intelligence by skillfully "extending" our minds into the world around us. It provides a new perspective on how to work, learn, and create more effectively by leveraging the full range of human cognitive capacity.
