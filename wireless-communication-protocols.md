# Wireless Communication Protocols

Wireless protocols like Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Wi-Fi have untethered automated systems. This allows for flexible placement of [[sensor-technology-in-automation|sensors]] and [[electronic-actuators|actuators]] without the need for expensive and intrusive wiring, greatly simplifying the deployment of automation in existing structures. These protocols are essential for the [[iot-impact-on-domestic-automation|Internet of Things]].
