# Bottom-Up Structure and Emergent Outlines

Unlike traditional writing methods that rely on top-down outlines, Doto advocates for a bottom-up approach where structure emerges from the notes themselves. As permanent notes are linked and clustered based on their conceptual relationships, natural groupings and sequences begin to appear. These clusters can then be used to generate "emergent outlines" for articles, books, or other projects.

This emergent structure is more flexible and resilient than a predetermined outline. It allows the writer to follow the natural flow of their ideas and discover connections they might have otherwise missed. By trusting the process of linking and clustering, writers can let their knowledge base guide them toward the most logical and compelling way to present their work.
