# Miniaturization of Electronics

The ongoing miniaturization of electronic components, driven by Moore's Law, has been a primary catalyst for mobile and wearable automation. As circuits become smaller and more efficient, they can be embedded into increasingly compact devices, enabling autonomous systems to operate in spaces previously unreachable by mechanical counterparts. This trend has significantly contributed to the [[cost-reduction-of-electronics|cost reduction of electronics]].
