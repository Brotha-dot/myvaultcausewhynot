# Sensor Technology in Automation

Sensors act as the sensory organs of an automated system. By converting physical phenomena—such as temperature, light, pressure, or proximity—into electronic signals, they provide the necessary data for decision-making. Advances in sensor precision and response time directly correlate with the sophistication of automation, often feeding into complex [[feedback-loops-in-electronic-control|feedback loops]].
