# The Myth of Multitasking

Multitasking is neither efficient nor effective. In reality, we are just 'task switching,' which comes with a significant cognitive cost and increases the likelihood of errors.

To achieve extraordinary results, you must give your full attention to one thing at a time. The more things you try to do, the less you will accomplish in any of them.
