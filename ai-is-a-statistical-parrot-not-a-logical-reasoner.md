# AI is a statistical parrot, not a logical reasoner

Large language models predict the next most likely token based on training data. They do not "understand" logic, physics, or software engineering principles. Relying on them as logical authorities leads to "plausible-sounding" but logically flawed code that can be dangerously deceptive, often resulting in [[first-principles-thinking-is-the-antidote-to-ai-hallucinations|hallucinations]].
