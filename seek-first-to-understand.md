# Habit 5: Seek First to Understand, Then to be Understood

Most people listen with the intent to reply, not to understand. Empathic listening involves listening with the eyes and heart as well as the ears.

By truly understanding another person's perspective before trying to present your own, you build trust and open the door to creative, win-win solutions that neither party could have found alone.
