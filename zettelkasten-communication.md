# Zettelkasten Communication

A Zettelkasten is often described as a "thinking partner" or a "dialogue partner." By linking notes and revisiting them over time, you engage in a conversation with your past selves. The system can "surprise" you by revealing unexpected connections between ideas captured at different times.
