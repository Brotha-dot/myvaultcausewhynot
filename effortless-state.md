# The Effortless State

The Effortless State is a mental condition where you are physically rested, emotionally calm, and mentally focused. It is the foundation for making hard things easier.

When we are stressed and exhausted, even simple tasks feel difficult. By prioritizing our state of being first, we can approach our work with a sense of lightness and clarity.
