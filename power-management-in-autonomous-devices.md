# Power Management in Autonomous Devices

Effective power management is essential for untethered automated devices. Electronic systems that can intelligently sleep and wake, or harvest energy from their environment, enable long-term autonomous operation without manual battery replacement or recharging. This is especially critical for devices using [[wireless-communication-protocols|wireless communication]].
