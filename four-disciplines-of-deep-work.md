# The Four Disciplines of Deep Work

To implement deep work, Newport suggests four disciplines: 1. Focus on the Wildly Important, 2. Act on Lead Measures (like time spent in deep work), 3. Keep a Compelling Scoreboard, and 4. Create a Cadence of Accountability.

These disciplines shift deep work from a vague intention to a concrete practice. By tracking the time spent in deep work rather than the outcome, you focus on the process that produces the results.
