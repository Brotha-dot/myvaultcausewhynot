# Deep work is interrupted by the constant ping of AI suggestions

The flow state required for complex problem-solving is fragile. Continuous, unsolicited AI suggestions (like ghost text) can break this focus, forcing the developer to constantly evaluate and dismiss small interruptions instead of staying immersed in the problem. This friction disrupts the [[mental-models-must-be-built-manually-to-be-durable|mental model building]] necessary for deep understanding.
