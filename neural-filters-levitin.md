# Neural Filters Levitin

The brain uses neural filters to prioritize information and ignore distractions. In an information-rich environment, these filters can become clogged, leading to sensory overload. Understanding how to support these filters (e.g., by reducing environmental noise) is crucial for maintaining focus and cognitive health.
