# Deep Work vs. Shallow Work

Deep work consists of professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit. Shallow work, by contrast, is non-cognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted.

While shallow work is necessary to keep the lights on, deep work is what produces massive results and rare skills. Most knowledge workers are drowning in shallow work, losing the ability to go deep.
