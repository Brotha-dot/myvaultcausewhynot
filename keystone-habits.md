# Keystone Habits and Small Wins

Keystone habits are those that, when changed, start a chain reaction that shifts other patterns in your life. For many, exercise is a keystone habit because it often leads to better eating and more productivity.

These habits provide 'small wins' that build a sense of confidence and momentum. By focusing on a single keystone habit, you can transform your entire lifestyle without trying to change everything at once.
