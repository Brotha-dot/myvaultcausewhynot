# Mastering Syntopical Reading

Syntopical reading is the most complex and difficult of all the levels of reading. It requires the reader to identify a specific subject and then find and read all the relevant books on that subject. The reader's task is not to understand each book in its own terms, but to use the books to answer a specific set of questions or to explore a particular problem.

The key to syntopical reading is the creation of a "synthetic vocabulary"—a set of terms that allows the reader to translate the different authors' idiosyncratic languages into a common framework. This allows for a direct comparison of their views. Syntopical reading is not about finding the "truth" in any one book, but about identifying the "controversy" or the range of opinions on a subject, leading to a more comprehensive and balanced perspective.
