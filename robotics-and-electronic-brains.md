# Robotics and Electronic Brains

Modern robotics is the fusion of mechanical engineering and electronic intelligence. The "brain" of a robot consists of complex electronic circuits that process [[sensor-technology-in-automation|sensor data]] and execute motion planning in real-time. Without high-speed electronics, the fluid and adaptive movements of modern robots would be impossible. [[electronic-actuators|Actuators]] serve as the physical interface for these electronic brains.
