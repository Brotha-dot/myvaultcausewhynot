# Externalizing Logic

Externalizing logic through writing allows you to see the gaps and contradictions in your thinking. When ideas are trapped in your head, they often feel more coherent than they actually are. Putting them on paper (or a digital note) subjects them to a level of scrutiny that leads to clearer and more robust thinking.
