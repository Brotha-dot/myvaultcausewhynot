# The Salami Slice Method

A major reason for procrastination is that big tasks seem overwhelming. The 'Salami Slice' method involves breaking a large task into tiny, bite-sized pieces.

You then resolve to do just one 'slice' at a time. Often, once you start, you'll find the momentum to do several more, eventually finishing the whole 'salami' without feeling the weight of the entire project.
