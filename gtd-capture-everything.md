# Capturing Everything to Reduce Anxiety

The first step of GTD is capturing every "open loop"—anything that has your attention—into a trusted inbox. This practice prevents the brain from constantly re-processing unfinished business, a phenomenon known as the Zeigarnik effect.

Capture tools should be as low-friction as possible. Whether it's a notebook, a voice recorder, or a digital app, the goal is to get the thought out of your head immediately so you can return to what you were doing.
