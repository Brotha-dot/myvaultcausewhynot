# The War for Your Attention

Tech companies are not neutral tools; they are designed to be addictive to maximize engagement and profit. Recognizing that you are in an asymmetric war for your attention is the first step to reclaiming it.

By treating these tools as something to be used for specific purposes rather than as a default state of being, you shift the power balance back in your favor.
