# Active Sorting Levitin

Active sorting is the process of organizing items at the moment of encounter or shortly thereafter. This prevents the accumulation of "clutter" and ensures that information is placed in the correct context while its relevance is still fresh. It's a proactive approach to maintaining the integrity of an organization system.
