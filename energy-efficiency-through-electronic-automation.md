# Energy Efficiency through Electronic Automation

Electronic automation systems significantly optimize energy consumption. By using real-time data to control heating, lighting, and industrial motors, these systems ensure that energy is only used when and where it is needed. This precision reduces waste and is a key driver in the transition to more sustainable living and manufacturing, often leveraging [[iot-impact-on-domestic-automation|IoT interconnectedness]].
