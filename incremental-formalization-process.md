# Incremental Formalization Process

Incremental formalization is the strategy of slowly adding structure to information as it becomes more important. You don't need a perfect system on day one; you can start with messy, unstructured notes and gradually refine them through tagging, linking, and rewriting as you revisit them. This prevents "pre-optimization" and keeps friction low.
