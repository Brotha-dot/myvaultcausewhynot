# Microcontrollers in Smart Homes

Microcontrollers are the "tiny brains" behind domestic automation. These low-cost, low-power integrated circuits enable everyday objects—from thermostats to light bulbs—to process information and respond to user input or environmental changes. The ubiquity of microcontrollers is what makes the "smart" in smart homes possible.
