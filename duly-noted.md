# Duly Noted

Duly Noted explores how to design personal information environments that support thinking and creativity. Jorge Arango applies principles of information architecture to personal note-taking, emphasizing that notes are not just static records but dynamic spaces that we inhabit and navigate. The book provides a framework for building a "thinking environment" that is scalable, resilient, and deeply integrated with one's personal and professional life.
