# Reclaiming High-Quality Leisure

Digital minimalists replace passive digital consumption with 'high-quality leisure'—activities that require effort and produce something of value, like wood carving, playing an instrument, or social interaction.

Low-quality leisure, like scrolling through feeds, provides a quick hit of dopamine but leaves you feeling empty. High-quality leisure provides lasting satisfaction and builds character.
