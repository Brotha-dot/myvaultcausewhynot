# The Critical Importance of the Weekly Review

Without regular maintenance, any productivity system will eventually collapse. The Weekly Review is the "glue" of GTD, where you clear your head, process your notes, and ensure every project has at least one current next action.

This ritual allows you to see the "big picture" and ensures your day-to-day actions align with your larger goals. It provides the confidence necessary to stay relaxed and focused during the busy work week.
