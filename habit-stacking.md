# The Power of Habit Stacking

Habit stacking involves anchoring a new habit to an existing one: 'After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT].' This leverages the neural pathways already established in your brain.

By creating a chain of habits, you reduce the mental energy required to decide what to do next. The existing habit serves as a powerful cue for the new behavior.
