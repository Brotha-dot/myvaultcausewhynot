# The Necessity of a Shutdown Ritual

A shutdown ritual is a formal end to the workday where you review your tasks, update your plan, and say a specific phrase (like 'shutdown complete'). This signals to your brain that it is safe to stop thinking about work.

Strictly enforcing this boundary allows for true rest, which is essential for replenishing the cognitive energy required for the next day's deep work.
