# Living with Purpose, Priority, and Productivity

Productivity is the fruit of priority, which is the child of purpose. Without a clear purpose, you won't know your priorities, and your productivity will be aimless.

By connecting your daily actions to your big 'Why,' you gain the motivation and clarity needed to stay focused on your ONE Thing, even when faced with distractions.
