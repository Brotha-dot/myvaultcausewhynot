# AI tools can act as a "Bypass" for critical thinking

The ease of generating code with AI can tempt developers to skip the "thinking" phase of programming. Instead of designing a solution from [[first-principles-thinking-is-the-antidote-to-ai-hallucinations|first principles]], they might iterate through AI suggestions until something "works," bypassing the critical analysis required for robust software design.
