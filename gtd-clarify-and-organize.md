# Clarifying and Organizing Your Inputs

Captured items are meaningless until you clarify what they are and what needs to be done about them. GTD asks: "Is it actionable?" If yes, what is the next physical action? If no, it goes to trash, reference, or a "someday/maybe" list.

Organization follows clarification. Actions are filed into context-based lists (e.g., @Computer, @Calls) rather than by project. This ensures that when you are in a specific context, you see all the work you can actually perform.
