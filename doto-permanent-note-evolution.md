# The Evolution of Permanent Notes

In a Zettelkasten, notes are not static; they are meant to evolve over time. Doto explains that as new information is added and new connections are made, existing permanent notes may need to be refined, expanded, or even split into multiple notes. This evolutionary process ensures that the knowledge base remains accurate, up-to-date, and aligned with the writer's current understanding.

The evolution of notes is facilitated by the use of "structure notes" or "index notes," which provide higher-level overviews of specific topics or themes. These notes act as entry points into the web of permanent notes, allowing the writer to see the "big picture" and identify areas that need more development. This continuous cycle of creation and refinement is what makes the Zettelkasten a truly dynamic and generative system.
