# The Law of Forced Efficiency

The Law of Forced Efficiency states: 'There is never enough time to do everything, but there is always enough time to do the most important thing.'

When we accept this, we stop trying to 'clear the decks' and instead focus on the vital few. The more you focus on important tasks, the more efficient you become at them, creating a virtuous cycle.
