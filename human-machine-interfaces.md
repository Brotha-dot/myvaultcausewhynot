# Human-Machine Interfaces (HMIs)

HMIs are the electronic bridge between humans and automated systems. Through touchscreens, voice recognition, or gesture control, these interfaces allow users to monitor and direct complex automation without needing to understand the underlying circuitry or code, often interacting with [[embedded-systems-in-appliances|embedded systems]] or industrial [[plcs-in-industrial-automation|PLCs]].
