# Designing a Buffer for the Unexpected

Essentialists acknowledge that the unexpected will happen. Instead of planning for the best-case scenario, they build in a 'buffer'—a margin of time and resources—to handle delays and surprises.

This practice reduces stress and ensures that a single mishap doesn't derail your entire project. A buffer is not waste; it is a strategic investment in reliability and peace of mind.
