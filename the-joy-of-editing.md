# The Joy of Editing Your Life

A good editor doesn't just cut words; they make the remaining words more impactful. Essentialists are the editors of their own lives, ruthlessly removing the trivial to make the vital shine.

This involves a constant process of reviewing your commitments and eliminating those that no longer serve your highest purpose. It is a proactive, rather than reactive, way of living.
