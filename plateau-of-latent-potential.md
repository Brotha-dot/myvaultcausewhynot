# The Plateau of Latent Potential

Small changes often appear to make no difference until you cross a critical threshold. This is the 'Plateau of Latent Potential,' where the results of your past efforts finally become visible.

Many people quit in the 'Valley of Disappointment' because they expect linear progress. In reality, habits are a compound interest of self-improvement; the biggest gains come late.
