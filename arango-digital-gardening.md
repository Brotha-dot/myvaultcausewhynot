# Digital Gardening and Incremental Design

Arango adopts the metaphor of "digital gardening" to describe the ongoing process of cultivating a personal knowledge base. Unlike traditional filing systems, which are often rigid and top-down, a digital garden grows organically through incremental design and continuous refinement. This approach acknowledges that our understanding of a topic evolves over time, and our note-taking system should reflect that evolution.

Incremental design allows us to start with "seed" notes—small, undeveloped ideas—and slowly grow them into more robust "evergreen" notes. By regularly revisiting and "weeding" our notes, we ensure that the system remains relevant and healthy. This process isn't just about cleaning up; it's a form of active processing that strengthens our understanding and reveals new connections between disparate ideas.
