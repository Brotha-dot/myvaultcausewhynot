# Habit 1: Be Proactive

Being proactive means taking responsibility for your life. It's the realization that between stimulus and response, you have the freedom to choose.

Proactive people focus their time and energy on their 'Circle of Influence'—the things they can actually change—rather than their 'Circle of Concern.' This shifts the focus from being a victim of circumstances to an architect of your own life.
