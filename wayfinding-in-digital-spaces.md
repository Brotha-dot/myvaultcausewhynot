# Wayfinding in Digital Spaces

Wayfinding is the process of using spatial and visual cues to navigate an environment. In a digital note system, wayfinding is facilitated by clear titles, consistent structures, and intentional links. Good wayfinding allows you to know where you are, where you've been, and where you can go next within your knowledge base.
