# AI excels at boilerplate but struggles with context-aware design

Standardized code patterns and boilerplate are perfect candidates for AI generation. However, high-level design requires an understanding of business logic, user needs, and long-term goals—context that AI lacks. Relying on AI for design often results in generic, ill-fitting solutions that lack [[intentional-coding-is-the-foundation-of-maintainable-software|intentionality]].
