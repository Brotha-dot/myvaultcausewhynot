# Gardening Metaphor Notes

The gardening metaphor highlights that note-taking is an ongoing, active process of cultivation rather than a one-time act of storage. A "garden" requires regular attention—planting (capturing), pruning (refining), and weeding (archiving)—to remain productive and healthy. It emphasizes growth and evolution over static organization.
