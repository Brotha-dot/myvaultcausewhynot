# The Power of Organizational Habits

Organizations have habits too, often born out of unwritten rules and long-standing routines. These habits can either make a company highly efficient or lead to disaster.

Duhigg uses the example of Alcoa, where focusing on a single habit (safety) transformed the company's culture and profitability. Leaders can use crises to reshape organizational habits for the better.
