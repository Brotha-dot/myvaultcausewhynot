# Habit 3: Put First Things First

Habit 3 is the physical creation—the practice of effective self-management. It involves organizing and executing around your most important priorities.

Covey introduces the Time Management Matrix, urging us to spend more time in Quadrant II: tasks that are important but not urgent (like planning and relationship building). This prevents the constant firefighting of Quadrant I.
