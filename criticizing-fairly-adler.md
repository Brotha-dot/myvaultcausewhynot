# Criticizing Fairly Adler

Fair criticism requires a complete understanding of the author's message before passing judgment. You must be able to say "I understand" before you say "I agree," "I disagree," or "I suspend judgment." Criticism should be based on identifying where the author is uninformed, misinformed, illogical, or incomplete.
