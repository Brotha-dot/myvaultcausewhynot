# Hierarchical Filing Levitin

Hierarchical filing systems organize information into nested categories (folders within folders). While powerful, they can become brittle if the hierarchy is too deep or rigid. Effective hierarchies are shallow and intuitive, allowing for quick navigation while minimizing the cognitive effort required to decide where a new item belongs.
