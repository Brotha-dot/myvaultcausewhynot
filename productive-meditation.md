# The Art of Productive Meditation

Productive meditation involves taking a period in which you are physically occupied but not mentally—like walking or showering—and focusing your attention on a single, well-defined professional problem.

The goal is to repeatedly pull your mind back to the problem when it wanders. This practice builds your 'concentration muscle' and often leads to breakthroughs that don't happen at a desk.
