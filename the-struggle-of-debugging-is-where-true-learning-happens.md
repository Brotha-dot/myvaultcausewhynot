# The struggle of debugging is where true learning happens

The process of finding and fixing a bug manually forces a developer to understand the system's state and logic deeply. When AI "fixes" the bug for you, you may solve the immediate problem but miss the opportunity to learn *why* it happened, leaving you vulnerable to similar issues in the future. This is why [[mental-models-must-be-built-manually-to-be-durable|manual understanding]] is so important.
