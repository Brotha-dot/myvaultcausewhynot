# Eat the Biggest, Ugliest Frog First

Your 'frog' is your biggest, most important task—the one you are most likely to procrastinate on. If you have two frogs, eat the ugliest one first.

By starting your day with your most difficult task, you build momentum and avoid the mental drain of dreading it all day. This habit alone can double your productivity.
