# Writing as a Method of Thinking

A core tenet of Doto's approach is that writing is not just a way to record thoughts, but a primary method of thinking itself. By externalizing our ideas through writing, we can inspect them, refine them, and connect them in ways that are impossible within the confines of our internal memory. The Zettelkasten serves as an extension of the mind, providing a workspace where complex logic can be mapped out and tested.

In this view, the act of creating a permanent note is an act of rigorous thinking. It requires the writer to clarify their understanding, define their terms, and justify their conclusions. This process forces a level of precision that internal monologue rarely achieves. Over time, the accumulated notes form a coherent system of thought that reflects the writer's evolving perspective and expertise.
