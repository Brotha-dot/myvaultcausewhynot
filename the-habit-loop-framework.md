# The Habit Loop Framework

The habit loop is a three-step neurological process: first, there is a cue, a trigger that tells your brain to go into automatic mode. Then there is the routine, and finally, the reward.

To change a habit, you must identify the cue and the reward, and then experiment with different routines that satisfy the same craving. You cannot extinguish a bad habit; you can only replace it.
