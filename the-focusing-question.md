# The Focusing Question

The core of the book is the Focusing Question: 'What's the ONE Thing I can do such that by doing it everything else will be easier or unnecessary?'

This question forces you to identify the lead domino—the one action that will have the greatest impact. By asking it repeatedly, you can align your daily tasks with your long-term goals.
