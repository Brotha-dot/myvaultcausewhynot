# The Essence of Essentialism

Essentialism is not about how to get more things done; it's about how to get the *right* things done. It doesn't mean just doing less for the sake of less. It is about making the wisest possible investment of your time and energy.

By forcing ourselves to apply more selective criteria for what is Essential, the disciplined pursuit of less allows us to regain control of our own choices about where to spend our precious time.
