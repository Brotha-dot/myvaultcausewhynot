# Mechanical to Electronic Automation Shift

The historical shift from purely mechanical automation (gears, cams, and levers) to electronic control has introduced unprecedented flexibility. Electronic systems can be reprogrammed without changing physical hardware, allowing a single machine to perform a vast variety of tasks with high precision, far surpassing the capabilities of [[reliability-of-solid-state-electronics|mechanical systems]].
