# Inspectional Reading Value

Inspectional reading is a disciplined form of skimming. It involves looking at the table of contents, index, and key chapters to get the "gist" of a book quickly. Its value lies in helping the reader decide if a book is worth a deeper (analytical) read and in providing a mental framework for that deeper reading.
