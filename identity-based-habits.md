# Identity-Based Habits

Most people focus on *what* they want to achieve (outcomes). Clear argues that the most effective way to change habits is to focus on *who* you want to become (identity).

Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to be. Instead of 'I want to run a marathon,' think 'I am a runner.' When your habits align with your identity, they become effortless.
