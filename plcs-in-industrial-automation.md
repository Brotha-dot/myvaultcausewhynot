# PLCs in Industrial Automation

Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) are the backbone of modern industrial automation. They replaced hard-wired relay logic with flexible, software-based control, allowing for rapid reconfiguration of manufacturing processes. Their ruggedized design ensures reliability in harsh environments, making them essential for everything from assembly lines to chemical plants, where [[real-time-processing-in-automation|real-time processing]] is a necessity.
