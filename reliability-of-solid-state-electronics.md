# Reliability of Solid-State Electronics

The reliability of automation has increased dramatically with the adoption of solid-state electronics. Unlike mechanical switches or relays, solid-state components have no moving parts to wear out, leading to longer lifespans and lower maintenance requirements for automated infrastructure. This is a key reason for the [[mechanical-to-electronic-automation-shift|shift from mechanical to electronic systems]].
