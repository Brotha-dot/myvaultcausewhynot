# Achieving Effortless Results

Effortless results are outcomes that continue to flow even when you are not actively working. This is achieved through systems, automation, and teaching others.

Instead of just getting a result once through brute force, look for ways to create 'residual results' that pay off over and over again. This is the difference between linear and exponential progress.
