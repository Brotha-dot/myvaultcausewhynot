# Craving: The Engine of Habit

A cue alone isn't enough to trigger a habit; there must also be a craving for the reward. Over time, the brain starts to anticipate the reward as soon as it sees the cue.

This neurological anticipation is what makes habits so hard to break. To create a new habit, you must deliberately cultivate a craving for the specific reward that the routine provides.
