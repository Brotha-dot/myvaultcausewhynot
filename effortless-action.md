# Making Action Effortless

Effortless action involves simplifying the process of getting things done. This includes defining what 'done' looks like, taking the 'first obvious step,' and making the process enjoyable.

Often, we overcomplicate tasks with unnecessary steps. By stripping away the complexity and focusing on the simplest path forward, we can achieve our goals with much less friction.
