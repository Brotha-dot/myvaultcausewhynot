# Interoception Awareness Paul

Interoception is the perception of internal bodily signals (e.g., heartbeat, breathing). Research suggests that people with higher interoceptive awareness are better at using their "gut feelings" to make accurate decisions and manage their emotions. Cultivating this awareness allows us to tap into an important source of embodied intelligence.
