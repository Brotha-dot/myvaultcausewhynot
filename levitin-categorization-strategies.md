# Categorization Strategies for a Cluttered World

Our brains are natural categorizers, but the sheer volume of information today requires more intentional strategies. Levitin explains that effective categorization is the key to findability and cognitive ease. By grouping related items and information into clear, distinct categories, we reduce the search space and make it easier for our brains to locate what we need.

Effective categorization also involves understanding the difference between "active" and "passive" organization. Active organization involves making deliberate choices about where things belong based on how they will be used. This might mean creating a "miscellaneous" category for items that don't fit elsewhere, or using a hierarchical structure to manage complex data. The key is to create categories that are intuitive and reflective of our actual workflows, making the system work for us rather than against us.
