# Skimming Techniques Adler

Effective skimming is not a random glancing at pages but a systematic process. It involves reading the preface, the table of contents, the index, and the first/last paragraphs of key chapters. This "pre-reading" provides a high-level map of the book and saves time by identifying the most important sections.
