# Pivoting Note Structure

Pivoting is the act of using a link to jump from one perspective to another. For example, moving from a "Project" note to a "Person" note linked within it. Designing your system for "pivotability" means creating multi-directional relationships that allow you to explore your knowledge from various angles and contexts.
