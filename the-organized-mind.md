# The Organized Mind

The Organized Mind explores how we can use neuroscience to better manage the overwhelming amount of information in our modern lives. Daniel J. Levitin explains how our brains evolved to process information and why we are so prone to cognitive overload and decision fatigue. The book provides practical strategies for offloading cognitive tasks to external systems, allowing us to regain focus, creativity, and mental clarity. It's a foundational text for understanding the biological basis of organization and personal productivity.
