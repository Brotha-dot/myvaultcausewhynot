# Electronic Actuators

Electronic actuators are the muscles of automation. They take electronic control signals and convert them into physical motion—whether it's opening a valve, moving a robot arm, or locking a door. The precision of electronic control allows for incredibly fine-tuned and repeatable physical actions, often guided by [[feedback-loops-in-electronic-control|feedback loops]].
