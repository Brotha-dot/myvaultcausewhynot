# The Art of Analytical Reading

Analytical reading is the most complete form of reading possible for a single book. Adler breaks it down into several stages, including classifying the book, outlining its structure, and "coming to terms" with the author by identifying their key words and how they use them. The goal is to move from a surface-level understanding to a profound grasp of the author's propositions and arguments.

A critical part of analytical reading is the ability to criticize a book fairly. Adler argues that a reader must understand a book completely before they can agree or disagree with it. This involves identifying the author's primary problems and determining whether they solved them. Analytical reading transforms the reader from a passive recipient of information into an active participant in a dialogue with the author.
