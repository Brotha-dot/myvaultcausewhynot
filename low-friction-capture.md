# Low-Friction Capture

Low-friction capture is essential for ensuring that fleeting ideas are not lost. The system must make it as easy as possible to record an idea the moment it occurs, without requiring complex categorization. Structure should be added later, during the "processing" or "gardening" phase, rather than at the moment of inspiration.
