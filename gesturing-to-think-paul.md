# Gesturing to Think Paul

Gesturing with our hands while thinking or speaking is not just for the benefit of the listener; it helps the speaker's brain process information. Gestures act as a form of "external scaffolding" that helps us hold and manipulate complex ideas, often revealing thoughts that we haven't yet put into words.
