# The State of Mind Like Water

The "Mind Like Water" concept in GTD refers to a state of mental clarity and preparedness, analogous to how water reacts to a pebble thrown into it: it reacts perfectly to the input, then returns to calm. By externalizing every commitment, you free up psychic energy for the task at hand.

When your mind is clear and your system is trusted, you can be fully present. You don't have to worry about what you might be forgetting, because you know it's captured in your trusted external system.
