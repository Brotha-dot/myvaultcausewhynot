# The Danger of Solitude Deprivation

Solitude deprivation occurs when you spend zero time alone with your own thoughts, constantly feeding your brain inputs from others. Modern smartphones have made it possible to eliminate solitude entirely.

Without solitude, we lose the ability to process emotions, solve complex problems, and build a stable sense of self. Reclaiming time without digital input is essential for mental health.
