# Embedded Systems in Appliances

Embedded systems are specialized electronic controllers built into larger products. In modern appliances like washing machines or microwave ovens, these systems manage complex cycles and user safety, providing a level of automation that was once reserved for industrial settings. These often rely on robust [[microcontrollers-in-smart-homes|microcontrollers]] for localized processing.
