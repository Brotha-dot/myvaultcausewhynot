# The Four Levels of Reading

Adler identifies four levels of reading, each building upon the previous one. The first level is "Elementary Reading," which involves basic literacy. The second is "Inspectional Reading," a systematic form of skimming that allows the reader to understand the book's structure and core message in a limited time. The third is "Analytical Reading," a thorough and demanding process of understanding the book in its entirety.

The fourth and highest level is "Syntopical Reading." At this level, the reader is not just reading a single book, but multiple books on the same topic, comparing their arguments and identifying the core issues at stake. This level of reading is aimed at achieving a deep, objective understanding of a subject by looking at it from various perspectives. Understanding these levels allows a reader to choose the appropriate depth of engagement for any given text.
