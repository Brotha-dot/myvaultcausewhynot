# The Power of the Graceful 'No'

Many of us say 'yes' out of social pressure or a desire to please. An Essentialist recognizes that every 'yes' to a non-essential task is a 'no' to something truly important.

Learning to say 'no' gracefully and firmly is a vital skill. It requires separating the decision from the relationship and realizing that being respected is more important than being liked in the short term.
