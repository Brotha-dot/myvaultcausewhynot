# The Digital Declutter Process

Newport recommends a 30-day 'digital declutter' where you take a break from all optional technologies. This isn't just a detox; it's a reset that allows you to rediscover what you actually value.

During the 30 days, you explore offline activities and hobbies. At the end, you selectively reintroduce only the tools that provide a significant, tangible benefit to your life.
