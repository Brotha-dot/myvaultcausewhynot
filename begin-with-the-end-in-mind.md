# Habit 2: Begin with the End in Mind

This habit is based on the principle that all things are created twice: first mentally, then physically. To be effective, you must have a clear vision of your destination.

Covey suggests writing a personal mission statement to define your values and goals. By knowing what truly matters most to you, you can ensure that your daily actions align with your long-term vision.
