# The Power of the Domino Effect

Success is sequential, not simultaneous. A single 2-inch domino can topple another domino 50% larger. By the 57th domino, it could reach the moon.

Extraordinary results happen when you line up your 'dominoes' and hit the first one every day. This consistency over time leads to massive, exponential growth.
