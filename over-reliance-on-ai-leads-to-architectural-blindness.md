# Over-reliance on AI leads to architectural blindness

AI tools often suggest solutions that are local to the current file or function. If a developer accepts these suggestions without considering the broader system, they risk creating a fragmented architecture where individual parts work but the whole lacks coherence and long-term maintainability. This [[over-reliance-on-ai-leads-to-architectural-blindness|architectural blindness]] is a major risk of AI-heavy workflows.
