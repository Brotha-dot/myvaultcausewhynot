# Define 'Done' for the Day

To avoid the trap of endless work, define a 'Done' list for each day. Once you finish these tasks, you are officially done, regardless of how much time is left.

This prevents the psychological burden of a never-ending to-do list and allows you to enjoy your leisure time without guilt, which in turn prepares you for the next day.
