# Personal Information Architecture

Personal Information Architecture (PIA) is the practice of organizing and structuring your personal digital space to make information findable and usable. It applies professional IA principles—like organization, labeling, and navigation—to the scale of an individual's life. Effective PIA reduces the cognitive effort required to find and utilize your own knowledge.
